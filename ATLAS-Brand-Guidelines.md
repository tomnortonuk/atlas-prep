## Brand Overview

**ATLAS** is a national health and care data intelligence platform that brings together data from across acute, community, mental health, social care, emergency services, and public health into one comprehensive, navigable system.

### Brand Positioning
- **Primary:** The definitive reference for population health and care data
- **Tone:** Modern guide—professional but approachable, confident, clear
- **Audience:** Dual-mode platform serving both analytical deep-dive users and executive decision-makers

### Core Brand Values
1. **Comprehensive** - Every sector, every insight, one view
2. **Trusted** - Objective, evidence-based, authoritative
3. **Clear** - Complex data made navigable and actionable
4. **Modern** - Forward-thinking, accessible, user-focused

## Logo System

### Primary Logo

The ATLAS logo consists of a geometric icon mark paired with the wordmark.

**Icon Concept:**
A geometric composition suggesting:
- **Interconnected nodes** representing multiple data sources
- **Grid/map structure** evoking cartographic reference
- **Layered planes** showing depth and comprehensive coverage
- **Central anchor point** (atlas/reference concept)

**Geometric Construction:**
```
The logo is built on a 24×24 grid system:
- Three overlapping hexagonal/rectangular planes
- Central connection point
- Asymmetric balance suggesting dynamic data flow
- Clean, modern geometric forms
```

**Wordmark:**
- Font: Inter Bold
- Letter spacing: -0.02em
- All caps: ATLAS
- Color: Deep Teal #0A5C5F or Charcoal #2C3E42 (context dependent)

### Logo Variations

**1. Primary Horizontal**
- Icon left, wordmark right
- Use: Main navigation, headers, documents

**2. Stacked**
- Icon top, wordmark below
- Use: Square spaces, social media, mobile app icon

**3. Icon Only**
- Use: Favicon, app icons, when brand is established

**4. Wordmark Only**
- Use: Text-heavy contexts, legal documents

### Color Variations

**Light Backgrounds:**
- Primary: Deep Teal icon + Charcoal wordmark
- Alternative: All Deep Teal

**Dark Backgrounds:**
- Primary: White icon + White wordmark
- Alternative: Amber accent icon + White wordmark

### Minimum Sizes
- Horizontal logo: 120px wide minimum
- Stacked logo: 80px wide minimum
- Icon only: 32px minimum (favicon: 16px, 32px, 48px)

### Clear Space
Maintain clear space equal to the height of "A" in ATLAS around all sides

## Logo Usage Guidelines

### Do's
✓ Use approved logo files
✓ Maintain proper clear space
✓ Use on approved background colors
✓ Scale proportionally
✓ Use high-contrast combinations

### Don'ts
✗ Distort or stretch the logo
✗ Change colors outside brand palette
✗ Add effects (shadows, gradients not in original)
✗ Rotate the logo
✗ Recreate or modify the geometric icon
✗ Place on busy backgrounds without container

## Brand Voice

### Tone Characteristics
- **Clear, not complex:** Simplify without dumbing down
- **Confident, not arrogant:** Authoritative but accessible
- **Helpful, not prescriptive:** Guide, don't dictate
- **Modern, not trendy:** Professional and current, not fleeting

### Writing Style
- Use active voice
- Front-load key information
- Avoid jargon; explain technical terms when necessary
- Use "you" to address users directly
- Be specific with data and metrics
- Use parallel construction for lists

### Example Comparisons

❌ **Too Complex:**
"ATLAS leverages a federated data architecture enabling multi-dimensional analytical paradigms across heterogeneous care delivery modalities."

✅ **Clear:**
"ATLAS brings together data from across health and care sectors, helping you analyse trends at any level—from individual organisations to national systems."
