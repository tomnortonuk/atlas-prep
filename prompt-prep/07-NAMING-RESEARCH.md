# ATLAS Naming Research & Brand Selection

Complete documentation of the naming research process and competitive analysis.

---

## Initial Requirements

The platform name needed to:
1. **Include "health" must also mention "care"** - reflecting full system scope
2. **Not be NHS or Trust-specific** - covers independent sector, LAs, etc.
3. **Avoid conflicts** with existing platforms in the space
4. **Be memorable** and easy to communicate
5. **Inspire trust** and confidence
6. **Avoid existing system names** (e.g., SPINE, SUS)

---

## Initial Suggestion: DASH

**Expansion Options:**
- Data Aggregation and System Health
- Dynamic Analytics and System Hub
- Data Analytics for System Health

**Why It Was Appealing:**
- Short, punchy, memorable
- NHS loves acronyms
- "Dashboard" association
- Action-oriented

## DASH Conflict Research

### Major Conflicts Discovered

#### 1. **Edge Health DASH** ⚠️ **CRITICAL CONFLICT**

**What:** Direct competitor - commercial UK NHS analytics platform
**Full Name:** Data, Analytics and Software Hub
**Owner:** Edge Health Ltd (UK)
**Offering:**
- NHS data ingestion and curation (RTT, cancer waits, public data)
- Automated monthly data refreshes
- Power BI dashboards
- Azure + Databricks pipelines
- £55k-110k annual pricing

**Market:** UK NHS trusts and ICBs
**Status:** Active commercial product on Gov Digital Marketplace

**Verdict:** ❌ **This is a direct competitor doing almost exactly what we're building**

---

#### 2. **DASH Risk Assessment** ⚠️ **HIGH RECOGNITION CONFLICT**

**What:** Domestic Abuse, Stalking and 'Honour'-based violence risk assessment
**Owner:** SafeLives (UK charity)
**Usage:**
- Mandatory tool for police, NHS safeguarding teams, IDVAs
- Used in Multi-Agency Risk Assessment Conferences (MARAC)
- Every NHS trust safeguarding team uses this
- Embedded in UK health and social care culture

**Recognition:** Extremely high in NHS - "DASH" = safeguarding for most staff

**Verdict:** ❌ **Would cause confusion; too embedded in NHS culture**

---

#### 3. **UK Gov DaSH Portal** - Moderate Conflict

**What:** Discontinuations and Shortages portal
**Owner:** DHSC (Department of Health and Social Care)
**Usage:**
- Pharmaceutical companies report medicine shortages
- Email: dash@dhsc.gov.uk
- Specialist pharmacy/supply chain audience

**Verdict:** ⚠️ **Moderate conflict - different audience but government-owned**

---

#### 4. **Plotly Dash** - Framework Conflict

**What:** Major Python/JavaScript framework for analytical web applications
**Owner:** Plotly (US)
**Usage:**
- Very popular in data science community
- NHS orgs use it (Somerset NHS FT, NHS England)
- Technical audiences assume you mean the framework

**Verdict:** ⚠️ **Would confuse technical users**

---

#### 5. **Other Healthcare DASH Acronyms**

- **The Joint Commission DASH:** Data Analytics for Safe Healthcare (US)
- **Radix Health DASH:** Registered trademark for medical scheduling (US)
- **DASH IG:** Data Aggregation Services for Health - FHIR standard
- **Data Across Sectors for Health:** US multisector initiative
- **NICHD DASH:** Research data and specimen hub (US)

**Verdict:** ⚠️ **Multiple collisions in healthcare analytics space**

## Final Selection: ATLAS

### Why ATLAS Won

**1. Zero Conflicts**
- ✅ No UK healthcare analytics platform
- ✅ No NHS system name
- ✅ No government portal
- ✅ Only one GP practice uses the name (not a platform)

**2. Perfect Metaphor**
- **Cartographic reference:** Maps and navigation
- **Comprehensive:** Atlas contains everything in one place
- **Authoritative:** Reference material, trusted source
- **Guidance:** Helps you navigate complexity

**3. Meets All Requirements**
- ✅ Not NHS/Trust-specific
- ✅ Memorable single word
- ✅ Inspires trust
- ✅ Easy to explain: "An atlas helps you navigate - ATLAS helps you navigate health and care data"

**4. Brand Potential**
- **Strong visual:** Layered maps, interconnected data
- **Logo:** Geometric planes representing data integration
- **Tagline:** "Navigate the complete picture"

**5. Scales Appropriately**
- Works at every level: national, regional, local
- Suggests completeness without being overwhelming
- Professional but not corporate
- Modern but not trendy

- Multiple layers = Multiple data sources
- Overlapping planes = Data integration
- Geometric precision = Analytical rigor
- Cartographic markers = Navigation reference

- Bridges blue (trust) and green (health/growth)

- Modern, professional, data-oriented
- Used by Gov.uk (secondary color)

## Lessons Learned

1. **Research depth matters:** Initially liked DASH, research revealed major conflicts
2. **NHS culture is specific:** Names embedded in NHS (like DASH safeguarding) carry strong associations
3. **Check commercial landscape:** Direct competitors are more problematic than technical framework names
4. **Single-word names work:** Don't force acronyms; let metaphor do the work
5. **Government namespaces:** Avoid anything used by NHSE, DHSC, UKHSA to prevent confusion
